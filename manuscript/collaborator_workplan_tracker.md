## 3. Decision record: "Methodological considerations" subsection (item 3 / KF26 vs. KF32)

- **Option B — fold into main text, delete the standalone subsection.** Delete the subsection; trim the Discussion roadmap sentence to drop the promise of it; fold the manipulative-experiments/genotype point into the existing common-garden paragraph; fold the tissue-preservation point into the end of the "Consistent taxa" section. *Pros:* resolves the KF26/KF32 conflict outright; each point lands next to the result it's most relevant to. *Cons:* touches three edit sites instead of one.

**Chosen: Option B.** Applied: deleted the standalone subsection; trimmed the roadmap sentence; folded the genotype/FEF-causality point into the common-garden paragraph; folded the tissue-preservation point into the "Consistent taxa" section (now "Additional finding," see §4). Follow-up completed: trimmed the now-redundant common-garden/tissue-preservation restatement in the Conclusions paragraph, pointing readers to the Discussion instead; fixed a stray typo ("taht" → "that") in the same sentence.
